# How we know

This area owns the public explanation of how SellWhatPeopleWant investigates and
explains a business question. The route stays `/methodology` for compatibility, but
the reader-facing name is “How we know.” Internal research vocabulary does not belong
in the public teaching layer.

The page starts with ordinary human questions, teaches through a clearly fictional
worked example, and opens deeper paths into question formation, concept meaning and
history, evidence limits, arithmetic, disagreement, original sources, and unresolved
work. A reader who stops after the first layer should understand the decision; a
reader who continues should be able to reconstruct how the present answer was made.

This area does not define the machine-readable case contract, certify conclusions,
or replace qualified legal, safety, technical, accounting, or local judgment.
Preserve progressive disclosure, exact source links, visible open ends, accessible
semantic HTML, and the restrained paper-and-ink editorial design.
