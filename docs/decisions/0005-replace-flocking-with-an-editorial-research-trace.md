# ADR 0005: Replace flocking with an editorial research trace

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-08-19
- Owners: Project maintainers
- Supersedes: Opening-chapter portion of ADR 0003
- Superseded by: ADR 0006

## Context

The research correction replaced the agent-first demand graph with a human-facing
Opportunity Case. Flocking was selected to represent individual requests becoming
collective intelligence, so it now communicates a product loop the lab explicitly
no longer claims. The retained fluid chapter can still represent fragmented evidence
becoming an inspectable shared case without carrying that obsolete meaning.

## Decision

Remove the three.js bird renderer from the landing page and replace the opening with
an editorial research dossier. Use D3 to draw a restrained, explicitly illustrative
method trace across candidate, triangulated, costed, and stress-tested stages. Label
the chart as fictional and not a market claim. Keep all product meaning in semantic
HTML and preserve the GPU-IO fluid chapter as the only decorative canvas.

## Alternatives considered

### Retheme the flock as research activity

The visual could be relabelled, but its convergence metaphor would still imply that
more individual signals naturally produce market truth. That is the exact inference
the research correction rejects.

### Replace both generative systems

Removing the fluid system would increase scope without solving a semantic defect. Its
meaning remains compatible with triangulation, and it is technically isolated.

### Show invented opportunity data

Invented market numbers would make the page look finished before the first case meets
the research standard. A clearly labelled method trace demonstrates the interface
without creating false evidence.

## Consequences

- The initial bundle adds tree-shaken D3 modules and removes three.js.
- The opening becomes quieter, sharper, and more publication-like.
- Fictional chart values must remain visibly labelled and must never be reused as
  evidence, a score, or a prediction.
- ADR 0003 continues to govern the retained fluid renderer's isolation and fallback.

## Validation

Keep this decision while readers can distinguish the lab's promise, claim types,
research stages, strongest counter-case, and current no-case-yet status without
mistaking illustrative graphics for real research.
