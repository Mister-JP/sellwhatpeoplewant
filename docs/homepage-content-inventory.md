# Home and About content inventory

## North Star claims

- SellWhatPeopleWant is a human-facing opportunity-research lab for people deciding
  what to build, import, distribute, repair, or provide.
- The primary product is the Opportunity Case: a bounded, evidence-backed,
  continuously correctable argument about whether a business may work in a named
  place, for named customers, under named conditions.
- The unit of value is a decision improved: an opportunity rejected before wasting
  money, tested more cheaply, adapted locally, or pursued with clearer economics.
- A case exposes both the opportunity and the strongest argument against it.
- Statements, reviews, searches, interviews, purchases, and shortages are signals,
  not objective demand. Their provenance and selection limits remain visible.
- Cases separate observed fact, sourced estimate, calculation, interpretation,
  hypothesis, and recommendation.
- Readers can edit material assumptions and compare base, upside, downside, and
  break-the-business scenarios.
- Global research never turns a cross-market analogue into a local fact without
  justifying transportability.
- Participation improves specific claims, sources, assumptions, scenarios, local
  inferences, or unresolved questions. Popularity is not evidence.
- Agents help research and consume structured cases, but humans remain the primary
  audience and final decision-makers.
- The North Star is whether a case made an opportunity understandable and testable.

## Present-stage facts

- No real Opportunity Case has been published in the public library yet.
- The first stage is one geography, one bounded opportunity family, and a small
  portfolio of deeply researched, visually excellent cases.
- Open collaboration, implementation services, financing information, and a
  real-world business simulator are later stages rather than current features.
- The homepage defines a product contract and research standard; it does not claim
  customers, adoption, validated opportunities, or realized business outcomes.

## Page outline

### Chapter one — the product and one tangible example

1. State that SellWhatPeopleWant researches business ideas before people risk money.
2. Define the transparent, interactive Opportunity Case as the product.
3. Show one explicitly hypothetical import case with required evidence, counter-case,
   and cheapest next test.
4. Take the product apart into its proposition, gap, market, execution, economics,
   counter-case, and next experiment.
5. Explain the five-stage research method: bound, observe, model, attack, and test.

A scroll-driven Three.js evidence world carries this chapter. Shape and color
distinguish evidence, assumptions, constraints, and unknowns. It remains decorative,
non-interactive, and explicitly does not represent market data or confidence.

### Editorial break

“An idea becomes useful when its assumptions become visible. A case earns trust
when the world can correct it.”

### Chapter two — how the work earns trust

6. Explain inspectable claim types, calculations, counterevidence, local inference,
   revision history, and commercial separation.
7. State why no individual signal establishes objective demand.
8. Establish that a useful case may conclude no or insufficient evidence.
9. Explain how public evidence, not popularity, changes a conclusion.
10. Make global ambition compatible with local inference.
11. Close honestly with the still-in-research case-library boundary.

The GPU-IO fluid simulation carries this chapter. It remains an isolated decorative
system with a static fallback and no product meaning hidden in pixels.
